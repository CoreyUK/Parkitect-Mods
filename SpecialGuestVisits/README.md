# Special Guest Visits

Adds occasional special visitors to Parkitect using normal, save-safe guest entities.

## Visit cadence

- The first visit begins after a random 1-3 gameplay minutes.
- Only one special visitor can be active at a time.
- A visit lasts up to three gameplay minutes.
- The next visit occurs after a random 5-10 minute quiet period.
- The same visitor type never appears twice in a row.

## Visitor types

- **Health Inspector:** fines nearby dirty shops or vomit, capped at three citations per visit.
- **Safety Inspector:** fines nearby broken rides or rides with underfunded maintenance, capped at two citations.
- **Thrill Reviewer:** a 0.75+ excitement ride can grant a two-minute popularity boost.
- **Influencer:** a 0.60+ excitement ride can grant a smaller 90-second popularity boost.
- **Park Auditor:** reviews the latest operating result and applies either an efficiency reward or additional audit fee.

A compact badge appears over the visitor while a special visit is active. Multiplayer remains disabled until synchronization is validated.
